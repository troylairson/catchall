Family Board

A private calendar display for one family's home.

Family Board shows a household's calendars, kids' activities, dinners and to-do lists on a screen at home and on the family's phones. It runs on the family's own home server. It isn't a public service and doesn't offer accounts to anyone outside the family.

With permission, it reads Google Calendar events so they can appear on the family's display. It never changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar.
