Privacy policy

Family Board · Last updated November 16, 2026

Family Board is a private app used by one family on its own home server. This page explains what it does with information from Google.

What it accesses

When a family member connects their Google account, Family Board asks for read-only access to their Google Calendar (the calendar.readonly permission). It reads the list of calendars and the events in them (title, time, place and description) for roughly the past two weeks and the next four months.

How it's used

  • Events are shown on the family's own wall display and phones, in the right person's column.
  • Family Board never creates, changes or deletes anything in Google Calendar.
  • The information isn't used for advertising, analytics or anything other than showing the family's schedule.

Where it's stored

Event copies and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the family's home server. The token is encrypted. Nothing is sent to the app's developer or to any other company. The data isn't sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Removing access

Disconnecting a Google account in Family Board's settings deletes its stored events and token. Access can also be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Family Board's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub repository.
